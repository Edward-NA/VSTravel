About Us

Learn more about VSTravel and our journey

Our Story

VSTravel was established in 2020 as a small travel agent in Jakarta with a vision to showcase the beauty of Indonesia to the world. What started as a passion project by a group of travel enthusiasts has now grown into one of Indonesia's leading travel agencies.

Our journey began with a simple mission: to provide authentic Indonesian travel experiences that go beyond the typical tourist attractions. We wanted to share the hidden gems, cultural richness, and natural wonders that make Indonesia truly special.

Our Journey

2020

Founded in Jakarta as a small local travel agent focusing on domestic tourism.

2021

Expanded our services to include international tourists and opened our second branch in Bali.

2022

Opened two new branches in Medan and Surabaya to better serve our growing customer base.

2023

Recognized as one of the leading travel agencies in Indonesia with a focus on quality service and authentic experiences.

Our Mission

To promote Indonesia's natural wonders, cultural heritage, and unique destinations while providing exceptional service and unforgettable experiences to our customers.

Our Vision

To become the premier travel agency for Indonesian tourism, known globally for our authentic experiences, personalized service, and commitment to sustainable tourism.
